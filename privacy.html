Privacy Notice

This notice explains what happens with any personal information that passes through The Roaming Lens website. We've tried to keep it honest and straightforward — the site is deliberately simple, and so is the data picture.

Who we are

The Roaming Lens is operated by Anne Peters, a sole trader based in the United Kingdom. If you have any questions about this notice, contact us at annepeters86@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

This website does not collect, store, or process any personal data directly. There are no sign-up forms, no accounts, no contact forms, and no tracking of individual visitors.

If you use the "Request this image" button or the batch licensing request in any gallery, your own email client opens and you send a message directly to us. That email — including your name, email address, and whatever you write — arrives in our Gmail inbox and is handled like any other email correspondence. We use it solely to respond to your enquiry. We don't add you to a list, we don't pass your details to anyone else, and we don't use it for marketing.

We keep email correspondence for as long as is reasonable for a business record — typically a few years — and then it is deleted.

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies. We don't use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools. Fonts are served from our own files rather than Google's servers.

Cloudinary, which delivers the gallery images, operates a content delivery network that may log technical data such as IP addresses as part of normal CDN operation. This is incidental to image delivery and is governed by Cloudinary's own privacy policy.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical request data including IP addresses as part of standard server operation. This is governed by GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to request a copy of it, to ask us to correct or delete it, and to raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

In practice, if you've emailed us an enquiry and want us to delete that email, just ask and we will. Email annepeters86@gmail.com.

Changes to this notice

If anything material changes — for example if we add a contact form or client portal — we'll update this page and the date above.
